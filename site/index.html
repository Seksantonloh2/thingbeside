How it works

Five steps. No surprises.

From first chat to live website in about three weeks, with a fixed quote before anything starts.

Step 01Day 1 · 15 min
01

A free, no-pressure chat about your business, your customers and what your website needs to do. If we’re a good fit, you get a fixed quote and timeline.

Step 02Days 2–4
02

Brief

You fill in a simple brief covering your services, style and examples you like. Don’t have everything? I’ll help fill the gaps and write or polish your content.

Step 03Weeks 1–2
03

Design

I design your homepage first so you see the look and feel early. Once you’re happy, I design the remaining pages. Two rounds of revisions included.

Step 04Weeks 2–3
04

Build

I build your site in Framer with smooth animations, mobile-friendly layouts and SEO basics. You get a private preview link to check it on your own phone.
