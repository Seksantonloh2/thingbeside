Process

Simple, clear, and fast.

A proven five-step process that takes you from first chat to live website in weeks, with no surprises along the way.

Get in touchGet in touch
  1. 1
    Step 1 · Day 1 · 15 min

    Discovery call

    A free, no-pressure chat about your business, your customers and what you need your website to do. If we’re a good fit, I’ll send you a fixed quote and timeline.

  2. 2
    Step 2 · Days 2–4

    Brief

    You fill in a simple website brief covering your services, style, pages and examples you like. Don’t stress if you don’t have everything. I’ll help fill the gaps and write or polish your content.

  3. 3
    Step 3 · Week 1–2

    Design

    I design your homepage first so you can see the look and feel early. Once you’re happy with the direction, I design the remaining pages. This stage includes 2 rounds of revisions.

  4. 4
    Step 4 · Week 2–3

    Build

    I build your website in Framer with smooth animations, mobile-friendly layouts and SEO basics. You get a private preview link to check everything on your own phone and computer before it goes live.

  5. 5
    Step 5 · Week 3

    Launch and support

    I connect your domain, run final checks and put your site live. You’ll get a quick walkthrough on editing it yourself, plus 14 days of free support for any small fixes.
